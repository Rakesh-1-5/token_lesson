Tokens: how AI reads your text

AI models don't read letters or whole words. They read small pieces called tokens. Type below and watch your text get cut up.

Context window: the model's limited desk

A model can only look at a limited number of tokens at once. This limit is the context window. Everything it needs (your messages, its replies, instructions) must fit inside. Chat apps send the latest messages that fit and leave the oldest ones out, so the model never sees them.

Using this when you build an AI app

  • Count before you send. Use the model provider's tokenizer (for example, the tiktoken library for OpenAI models) to measure your prompt first.
  • Leave room for the answer. The window covers your input and the model's output together, so never fill it completely with input.
  • Trim or summarize old messages. Keep the newest turns word for word, and replace older ones with a short summary that keeps key facts like names and order numbers.
  • Keep instructions short. The system prompt is sent every time, so every extra word is paid for on every request.
  • Watch cost and speed. Providers bill per token, and more tokens usually means a slower reply.
